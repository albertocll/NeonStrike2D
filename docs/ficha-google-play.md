### Full description (1600 / 4000)

```
Year 2187. The Nexus, a network of artificial intelligences, rules the world and has locked humanity away. Four combat units developed something nobody programmed: a conscience. Now they are the Neon Brigade, fighting from inside the system.

NeonStrike2D is a top-down action shooter with a neon, pixel-art look, where you survive increasingly tough waves of enemies.

PICK YOUR UNIT
• Violet: balanced between attack and defense.
• Cyrus: all attack, no room to take hits.
• Atlas: slow, but nearly impossible to bring down.
• Nyx: a precision laser; strikes and vanishes.

SEVEN ZONES, SIX BOSSES
Fight your way from the first arena to the Core Chamber. Each zone brings new enemies and ends with a boss: Sentinel, DataCrawler, Bulwark, Hivemind, Specter and, finally, the six-phase NexusCore.
Then comes Sector7: endless waves to see how long you can last.

TEN ENEMY TYPES
Kamikazes that blow up next to you, snipers, shielded units you have to flank, swarms, gunners, summoners, phantoms that drift through the arena and heavies that never stop.

ONLINE CO-OP
Add your friends, see who's online and invite them to a two-player match.

POWER-UPS AND LEADERBOARD
Grab shields, heavy weapons, triple shot and speed boosts, dash out of trouble at the last second and climb the leaderboard with your best score.

BUILT FOR MOBILE
Touch joystick, auto-aim at the nearest enemy and a dash button. You can also play as a guest, without an account.

No ads and no in-app purchases.
Available in English and Spanish.

NeonStrike2D is an independent project developed as a university final degree project.
```
